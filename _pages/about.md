## About Me

I am currently a PhD candidate at the **Key Laboratory of Multimedia Trusted Perception and Efficient Computing**, Ministry of Education, Xiamen University, working with Prof. <a class="profile-person-link" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kAnv3SkAAAAJ&amp;hl=zh-CN">Cheng Wang</a> and Assistant Prof. <a class="profile-person-link" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cvS1yuMAAAAJ&amp;hl=zh-CN">Sheng Ao</a>.

I received my academic master’s degree in Computer Technology from Huaqiao University in 2023, advised by Prof. <a class="profile-person-link" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dPyyBxsAAAAJ&amp;hl=zh-CN">Xin Liu</a>. I am based in Xiamen, China. You can reach me at [judemihoo@gmail.com](mailto:judemihoo@gmail.com) or [mihoo@stu.xmu.edu.cn](mailto:mihoo@stu.xmu.edu.cn).
